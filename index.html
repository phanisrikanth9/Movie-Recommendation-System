      How it works

      Each film becomes a document of its genres, keywords, top three cast, director and plot. Documents are stemmed, weighted with TF-IDF and compared with cosine similarity.

      What changed

      Weighting the fields raised same-director matches in the top 10 from 15% to 82%, and genre overlap from 88.5% to 90.5%. Only each film's 20 nearest neighbours are stored: 3 MB instead of a 185 MB matrix.

      Data

      TMDB 5000 Movie Dataset from Kaggle. Everything runs in your browser; there is no server.
